Executive Summary

NAEA has the membership opportunity of a generation. Of the ~66,000 licensed Enrolled Agents in the United States, only a fraction are dues-paying members. The IRS FOIA roster makes the entire universe addressable — every name, city, and credential. What's missing is the engine that turns that public list into engaged prospects, podcast listeners, and renewing members.

This proposal delivers that engine: a phased enrichment pipeline that finds every EA's verified email and LinkedIn profile, a content + podcast SEO program that grows the EA Advocate Podcast 3–5x in twelve months, and a Decision Dashboard that turns activity into evidence for the board.

Five service tiers let NAEA scale into the program at the pace they're ready for — starting at $1,250/month and scaling to full association coverage. Every tier paces enrichment at 5,000 agents per month so each batch gets a full four-week campaign window before the next lands.

The recommended starting point is Tier 2 Foundation at 10,000 agents — matching the same audience NAEA would have reached through traditional direct mail this year, but delivering verified emails, LinkedIn profiles, and a content engine that compounds month over month instead of a one-time paper drop.

"The data is public. The infrastructure isn't. We're going to build the infrastructure, and use it month after month to turn the public roster into active members."

What a month looks like

Days 1–3: enrichment pipeline runs on this month's cohort. Output: verified emails, LinkedIn URLs, firm domains. Days 4–7: handoff to outreach — emails loaded into SMTP tool, LinkedIn URLs uploaded as Campaign Manager custom audience. Week 2: sequence one (intro) sends; LinkedIn ad campaign begins. Week 3: sequence two (value drop); ad creative adjusted based on early CTR. Week 4: sequence three (offer); conversion data pulled and briefed to NAEA. Day 28–30: decision — continue campaign on this cohort or move on. Next month's enrichment begins Day 1.

01

SEO foundation

03

04

Backlink machine

5 guest pitches per week to position NAEA practitioners in tax media. 10 listicle pitches per week to land in "best podcasts for tax pros" roundups — the primary signal LLMs use for recommendations.

05

Cross-platform syndication

Submission to Listen Notes, Podchaser, Goodpods (huge LLM training signals), plus the major directories. YouTube revival — 89 archive videos audited, retitled, and re-thumbnailed for search.

06

Conversion pipeline

Custom landing page (EA Resource Center) captures emails from podcast listeners. Email sequence nurtures toward membership. LinkedIn retargeting closes the loop on visitors who don't convert immediately.

Frequently asked

Can we upgrade tiers mid-engagement? Yes. Tier upgrades are billed prorated from the next month forward. Most clients start at Tier 1 or 2 and graduate to Tier 3 in Q2.

What if we want to pause? Each month is a clean stopping point. The pipeline state is preserved; resume at any later month with no re-enrichment cost.

Who owns the data? NAEA owns the enriched roster outright. Pipeline source code remains with the agency unless a Dashboard Buyout (Option A or B) is purchased.

What about GDPR / CAN-SPAM compliance? The IRS roster is public-record (FOIA). All outbound email follows CAN-SPAM (clear sender, physical address, opt-out). LinkedIn outreach uses the platform's compliant messaging tools.

What if a vendor (Outscraper, NeverBounce) raises prices? Data line on the invoice flexes with actual cost. Retainer is fixed.
