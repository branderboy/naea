AI Worker Execution (built into the layers)

Production runs on AI workers across the program:

  • Transcripts → Medium / Reddit / LinkedIn / Substack: the core engine. One AI worker per platform converts each episode transcript into a publish-ready post tuned for that platform's audience and algorithm (Layer 2).
  • Transcripts → episode page content: AI worker drafts the 2-sentence direct answer, FAQ section, and key takeaways for each website episode page (Layer 1).
  • Episode → social clip captions: AI worker drafts platform-specific copy for short-form video clips.
  • Episode → guest post draft: AI worker outlines and drafts the monthly guest post (Layer 4) from the episode being featured.

Human review and strategic decisions stay with the agency; AI handles the drafting load. This is how the program runs at $1,650/mo instead of $3,500+ at traditional agency labor rates.

How the email list feeds the podcast

The Enrolled Agent enrichment from Section 1 isn't just for direct outreach — it seeds the Substack newsletter (Layer 2) and feeds podcast subscriber growth. Verified emails go in as a starter subscriber list; LinkedIn URLs become ad audiences for podcast-awareness campaigns. Sections 1 and 2 compound when run together.
