# Guest Pitch Email Template

Send 5 of these per week. Track in a spreadsheet. Goal: each accepted guest = (a) episode, (b) backlink from their site/LinkedIn, (c) their audience hears the show.

---

**Subject:** Guest spot — EA Advocate Podcast (NAEA, ~[N]K downloads/episode)

Hi [First name],

I'm Brandy Spears, host of the EA Advocate Podcast — NAEA's official audio publication for the ~66,000 Enrolled Agents in the US.

I caught your recent [article / talk / LinkedIn post] on [specific topic]. The angle on [one specific thing they said] is exactly what our audience of practicing EAs wrestles with every week — and I'd love to have you on the show to dig into it.

Format is honest and low-friction:
- 35–45 min recorded conversation (Riverside, you don't install anything)
- We send you 3–5 questions a week ahead
- You get the audio + 3 short clips for your own social
- Episode lives on naea.org, Apple, Spotify, YouTube, plus our LinkedIn ad audience of [N] EAs

Recent guests include Terry Lemons (former IRS Communications), Colin Dunn, and Nayo Carter-Gray. Past episodes have ranked in Apple's top [N] for "tax" and been cited in [publication].

Two slots open in [month]. Are either of these workable on your end:
- [Date/time option 1]
- [Date/time option 2]

If timing's off, happy to schedule out. Either way, thanks for the work you're putting into [their field] — your [specific piece] genuinely moved my thinking on it.

Brandy Spears
Host, EA Advocate Podcast | NAEA
[email] | [LinkedIn URL]

---

## Variations

**For an academic / researcher**: Lead with "your [paper title] in [journal]" and emphasize that the show audience is practicing EAs who'd be applying their research.

**For a software / tech vendor**: Make clear it's NOT a paid sponsorship slot. Lead with what their product solves for EAs.

**For an IRS or Treasury official**: Lead with NAEA's role as the official EA association. Offer to share questions in advance and to send the transcript before publication.

**For a high-profile CPA/EA**: Lead with one specific thing they said publicly. Mention guest-list peers.

## Follow-up cadence

- Day 0: send pitch
- Day 5: short reply with one new angle ("Also wanted to add — Q2 audit volume is [stat], which connects directly to your point about [thing]")
- Day 14: final follow-up — "Closing the loop, no worries if timing's off"
- Don't pitch again for 6 months
