## Submission checklist per directory
For each, ensure:
- [ ] Title: "EA Advocate Podcast" (or your chosen exact name — use identical spelling everywhere)
- [ ] Description: first 250 chars contain "Enrolled Agent" and "NAEA"
- [ ] Cover art: 3000x3000 px, JPG or PNG, < 500KB
- [ ] Category: Business → Investing (primary); Education (secondary)
- [ ] Language: English
- [ ] Owner email: podcast@naea.org (consistent across all)
- [ ] Show website: https://naea.org/podcast (consistent)
