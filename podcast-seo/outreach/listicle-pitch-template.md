# Listicle Pitch Template — Getting Into "Best Podcasts For…" Roundups

This is the highest-leverage backlink work. Every listicle that includes EA Advocate is a citation LLMs use when asked "best podcast for tax pros." Aim for 10 pitches per week.

## How to find targets

Google these every month:
- `"best podcasts" "tax professionals" 2026`
- `"best podcasts" "CPAs" 2025`
- `"top podcasts" "enrolled agents"`
- `"best accounting podcasts"`
- `"podcasts for tax pros"`

Each result is a target. Bonus targets:
- Accounting Today, CPA Practice Advisor, Tax Pro Today annual roundups
- Tax software vendor blogs (Drake, ProConnect, UltraTax, TaxDome)
- Continuing-education provider blogs (Surgent, Gleim, Fast Forward Academy)

## Realistic conversion rate

- ~25% reply rate on cold pitches
- ~10–15% conversion to inclusion
- Of 100 pitches, expect 10–15 backlinks
- Each takes ~6 weeks from pitch to published update

This is the slow flywheel. Three quarters of consistent pitching gets you into 30–50 listicles, which is when LLMs start citing you reliably.
