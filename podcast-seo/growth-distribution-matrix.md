# Growth + Distribution Matrix — EA Advocate

| Growth Area | What Apple / Discovery Platforms Reward | Strategy | Execution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Distribution** | Consistent traffic + listener activity | Split distribution into Earned and Owned channels | Earned: news outreach, podcast pitches, guest appearances, industry mentions. Owned: Facebook, email, NAEA channels, website placements |
| **Podcast Rankings** | Download velocity, completion rate, subscriber count, review count, recency | Create coordinated ranking pushes around every episode launch | Send launch emails requesting downloads, follows, and reviews within the first 24–72 hours |
| **Subscriber Growth** | Repeat listeners + returning audience | Use automation once enrolled agent lists are built | Leverage Pardot for nurture flows, episode reminders, and "just in case you missed this" follow-up campaigns |
| **Listen Time & Retention** | Average listen duration + completion rate | Improve episode structure and usability | Add chapter markers, tighter episode segmentation, stronger intros, and clearer topic transitions |
| **Podcast SEO + AEO** | Search visibility, authority mentions, and citations in the sources LLMs pull from | Expand the indexing footprint for traditional search and get the show into the content LLMs cite when asked "best podcast for…" | List the show on Listen Notes, Podchaser, and Goodpods for SEO. For AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), land guest posts in "best tax podcasts" roundups — LLMs cite those lists when asked for podcast recommendations, so being on them is how the LLM learns the show exists |
