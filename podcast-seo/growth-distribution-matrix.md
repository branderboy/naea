# Growth + Distribution Matrix — EA Advocate

| Goal | Real Strategy (not platitudes) |
|---|---|
| **Downloads** | Apple ranks on download velocity in the first 48 hours. Strategy: pre-promote each episode to the email list 48 hours before drop, then push hard at launch time. Listicle placements in "best tax podcasts" roundups are the highest-leverage SEO play because LLMs cite those when asked for podcast recommendations. Niche directories (Listen Notes, Podchaser, Goodpods) feed AI training data — getting listed means LLMs know the show exists. |
| **Average Listening Time** | First 15 seconds is where most drop-off happens. Open cold with the hook, not intro music. Chapter markers don't just help navigation — they appear in Apple's UI as a value preview, which lifts completion. Topic clustering (releasing a 3-episode series on one theme) drives binge listening. |
| **Subscribers** | Subscribe CTAs alone don't move it. People subscribe when they hit a second episode they like. Real strategy: trigger a nurture sequence after Episode 1 that brings them back for Episode 2. Substack is a stronger subscribe driver than the podcast app for older demos because email beats push notifications. |
| **Podcast Ranking** | Apple weights download velocity, completion rate, subscriber count, review count, recency. You can't game completion (that's editorial). But you CAN prompt reviews in-episode — 80% of shows fail to do this. Win category ranking first (Business → Investing) before chasing Overall. |
| **Reach Growth** | Owned channels (FB Fanpage, YouTube) compound slowly but you control them. Rented channels (Apple, Spotify) move faster but you're at their mercy. Highest-ROI reach play: cross-promotion swaps with peer tax/finance podcasts — but that requires host-level relationships, not a buyable tactic. Wikipedia mention is the long-game holy grail for LLM entity recognition. |
