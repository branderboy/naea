# Growth + Distribution Matrix — EA Advocate

| Goal | Real Strategy (not platitudes) |
|---|---|
| **Reach Growth** | Two triggers running in parallel. Earned: pitch each episode as a news angle to tax/accounting press (Accounting Today, CPA Practice Advisor, Tax Notes) — third-party authority plus LLM-trainable citations. Owned: post the same hook as a native video on the NAEA Facebook fanpage with the episode link in the first comment. Earned compounds slowly but builds credibility; owned moves volume same-day to a warm audience. Run them on the same hook so they amplify each other. |
| **Podcast Ranking** | Single trigger: subscriber + review velocity in a concentrated 7-day window. Apple weights *velocity*, not totals, so one hard push beats a slow drip. Email the NAEA list once with a dated CTA ("subscribe + rate by Friday") and deep links straight to Apple's subscribe and review screens. Win the category rank (Business → Investing) before chasing Overall. |
| **Subscribers** | After the member list is built, leverage Parot to fire an automation flow at the existing audience. Sequence: Day 0 welcome + latest episode → Day 3 "the 3 most-played episodes" → Day 7 "subscribe in your app" with platform deep links → Day 14 re-engagement. People subscribe when they hit a second episode they like, not from a CTA — the flow exists to deliver that second episode automatically. |
| **Average Listen Time** | Add chapter markers to every episode — they show up in the Apple/Spotify UI as a value preview and lift completion rate (the biggest input to the ranking algorithm). For downloads, get listed on Listen Notes, Podchaser, and Goodpods — these feed AI training data and aggregator queries. The highest-leverage SEO play is guest-posting "best tax podcasts" roundups: LLMs cite those listicles when asked for podcast recommendations, so getting listed means ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity know the show exists. Backstop the whole thing with "just in case you missed this" emails to the agents list to recover lapsed listeners. |
