<li>Tax policy, IRS reform, and legislative advocacy</li>
<li>Niche practice development — creative artists, international clients, small business owners</li>
<li>The human side of client relationships: fear, trust, and emotional intelligence</li>
</ul>
<p>But don&#8217;t be surprised by the unique perspective each episode brings.</p>
